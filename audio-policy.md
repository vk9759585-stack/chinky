# CHINKY Audio Policy

CHINKY lets creators reuse audio that is marked reusable by its owner.

The built-in Music, Shorts, and Shayari Background catalog is generated for
CHINKY and marked `CHINKY Original • Royalty-free`. It contains no samples,
lyrics, melodies, or recordings copied from third-party songs. These catalog
tracks may be used in CHINKY creator content without attribution or royalty
payments. They may not be resold as a standalone stock-audio library.

- Upload only audio you created, own, licensed, or otherwise have permission to use.
- Original audio from a public Spark may be saved and reused when the audio is marked reusable.
- The original creator remains credited on reused audio.
- Users may save audio to their private Saved Audio library. Saving does not transfer copyright or ownership.
- Audio reported for copyright, safety, privacy, or legal reasons may be blocked or removed.
- Users must not upload copyrighted songs or recordings without the necessary rights.
- CHINKY may disable reuse for removed, private, restricted, or blocked media.
- Reusing audio does not grant permission to use a person's voice in a misleading, impersonating, or harmful way.

Report audio or copyright concerns from Help & Support.
