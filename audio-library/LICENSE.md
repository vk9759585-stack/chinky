# CHINKY Original Audio Library

The WAV recordings in this directory are original, procedurally generated
instrumental loops created for the CHINKY project. They do not sample or copy
third-party songs, performances, recordings, lyrics, or known melodies.

The CHINKY project owner grants CHINKY users a worldwide, royalty-free,
non-exclusive license to use these recordings in content created and shared
through CHINKY, including commercial creator content. Attribution is not
required. The recordings may not be resold or redistributed as a competing
standalone stock-audio library.

Catalog label: `CHINKY Original • Royalty-free`

The `shayari` tracks are instrumental backgrounds intended for a creator's own
spoken shayari or voice-over. No third-party spoken performance is included.
