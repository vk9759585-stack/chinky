# Chinky Child Safety Standards Policy

Chinky is committed to preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).

We strictly prohibit:

- Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
- Sexual exploitation of minors
- Grooming or predatory behavior
- Human trafficking
- Any content involving abuse of children

Our actions include:

- Reporting illegal content
- Permanently banning offending accounts
- Cooperating with law enforcement agencies
- Removing harmful content immediately

Users can report violations through the app.
